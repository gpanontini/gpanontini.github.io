Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Structured++ ("the app") is a personal planning app used by its developer. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar (scope calendar.events): reads your events to show your day and to give the assistant context, and creates events you ask for, including Google Meet links and invitations you approve.
  • Google Contacts (scopes contacts.readonly and contacts.other.readonly): looks up the email address of a person you name, so the app can ask you whether to invite them.
  • Structured planner: reads and updates your Structured tasks through Structured's official connector.
  • Your requests: the text you type or dictate. Dictation is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition on your device.

How data is used and shared

  • Your request, the relevant calendar events, contact search results and planner data are sent to Anthropic's Claude API so the assistant can plan the change. Anthropic processes this data under its commercial terms and privacy policy.
  • Calendar and contacts data are sent only between your device, Google, and Anthropic for the purpose above. The app has no servers of its own and keeps no copy of your data outside your device.
  • Sign-in tokens and the API key are stored only in your device's Keychain.
  • Data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.
  • Invitations are sent only after you explicitly confirm each recipient.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Sign out in the app's setup screen, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.
