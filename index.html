Structured++

A personal planning assistant for iPhone · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Purpose

Structured++ helps its user plan their day. From one place, by voice or text, the user can schedule meetings in their own Google Calendar (optionally inviting people they confirm), add tasks to the Structured daily planner, and reschedule tasks they didn't finish. The app uses Google Calendar to read and create the user's events, and Google Contacts only to find the email address of a person the user names when proposing an invitation.

Structured++ lets its owner plan by voice or text: add meetings to Google Calendar, add tasks to the Structured planner, and reschedule unfinished tasks. It is a personal tool built and used by one person; it is not sold or offered to the public.

How it works

  • You speak or type a request in the app. Speech is turned into text on your device by Apple's speech recognition.
  • The request is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which plans the change and replies in text.
  • With your permission, the app reads and creates events in your Google Calendar, looks up contacts' email addresses to suggest invites, and reads or updates your Structured tasks.
  • Calendar invitations are only sent after you explicitly tap "Send invite" for that person.
